# ASTRO BLOOM: GARDEN GUARDIANS

**在线体验 / Play online:** https://pipygames.com/games/astro-bloom-garden-guardians-baa7

The last garden planet is under siege by mossy meteor critters. You are the Sprout Guardian, orbiting the planet with a ring of leaf wings, aiming and firing seed shots to keep the garden shield alive. The game features 10 handcrafted levels, two boss battles, four permanent garden upgrades and an endless mode. Every level introduces a genuinely new rule: splitting meteors, three orbit lanes, a wandering black hole, meteor rain, crystal shields, portal storms, pollen turrets… At level 7 the Cinder King rises, and at level 10 the Meteor Monarch descends.

## 技术栈 · Tech stack

- 引擎 / Engine: **Three.js**
- 其他 / Also: Canvas 2D 图层
- 语言 / Language: 中文 + English
